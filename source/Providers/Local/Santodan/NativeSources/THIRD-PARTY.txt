NativePortDefinitions.json contains the achievement definition data for sixteen
ports, selected without changes to their entries from Achievement Watcher Next.
The first six were taken at:
https://github.com/Shirowwww/Achievement-Watcher-Next/blob/9069ff6a6b3ef59837f42ac13d0f79513918fef0/app/assets/recomp-ports.json
The remaining ten (smas, rr64, gdiffuser, nectar, nectar2, fh2azimuth, sanctuary,
issn, issnfl, deadnation) were taken at:
https://github.com/Shirowwww/Achievement-Watcher-Next/blob/f9903eb404f0eedf152144c576f197dcd96e2950/app/assets/recomp-ports.json
